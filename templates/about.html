About SkinNova

Novel Deep Learning Framework for Skin Cancer Detection

SkinNova is an explainable AI-powered clinical decision support system designed for skin cancer detection, lesion classification, visual explanation, confidence scoring, and clinical recommendation.

What is SkinNova?

AI-Assisted Skin Cancer Diagnosis with Explainability

SkinNova is not only a skin cancer classifier. It is a complete research-based AI framework that combines image preprocessing, lesion segmentation, hybrid deep learning, explainable AI, confidence estimation, and clinical decision support.

Seven-class lesion classification
Grad-CAM and LIME explanation
Hybrid EfficientNetV2 + ViT model
AI-assisted diagnostic report

Vision

To build a transparent and intelligent AI healthcare system that supports early skin cancer diagnosis and improves clinician trust through explainable decision-making.

Mission

To develop a robust deep learning framework capable of accurate skin lesion classification while providing visual explanation, confidence score, and clinical recommendation.

Problem Statement

Why SkinNova is Needed?

Skin cancer diagnosis depends heavily on clinical expertise and timely evaluation. Existing AI systems often lack transparency, making them difficult to trust in medical environments.

Delayed Diagnosis

Late detection can reduce treatment effectiveness and increase health risks.

Expert Dependency

Diagnosis depends on specialist availability and clinical experience.

Black-Box AI

Many deep learning models provide prediction without explanation.

Aim & Objectives

Research Goals of SkinNova

Aim

To develop a novel explainable deep learning framework for accurate skin cancer detection and diagnosis from dermoscopic images while providing transparent visual explanations and clinical recommendation.

Primary Objectives

  • Develop automated skin cancer detection system
  • Classify seven types of skin lesions
  • Improve diagnostic accuracy using hybrid AI
  • Support early clinical diagnosis

Explainability Objectives

  • Generate Grad-CAM heatmaps
  • Generate LIME explanations
  • Highlight responsible lesion regions
  • Increase clinician trust

Innovation Objectives

  • Remove hair and artifacts
  • Segment lesion before classification
  • Use cross-dataset validation
  • Generate AI diagnostic report
